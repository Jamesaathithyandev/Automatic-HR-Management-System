- **P1**: must-have for launch
- **P2**: add soon after
- **P3**: nice-to-have or advanced

## 1. Core platform

- Role-based access: employee, manager, HR, admin, founder (P1)
- Login with SSO (Google/Microsoft), 2FA, and password policies (P1)
- Audit log of who viewed or changed what (P1)
- Notification center plus email, Slack, and Teams alerts (P1)
- Global search across employees, documents, and policies (P2)
- Custom fields and custom workflows configurable by HR without code (P2)
- Multi-language and multi-currency support (P3)
- Multi-country and multi-entity support (P3)
- Public API and webhooks (P3)
- Dark mode and mobile PWA (P2)
